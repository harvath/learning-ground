Check it sticks

① Which layer do Mario Zechner, Armin Ronacher, and Peter Steinberger belong to, and why not "Labs"?

Tooling & services — they don't train foundation models. Zechner built pi (a harness), Ronacher co-founded pi's corporate home Earendil, and Steinberger built OpenClaw (a product built on top of pi). All three are building products that use a lab's models, not labs themselves.

② Which figure's own departures are directly cited (in A1) as contributing to a specific lab's flagship-model delay?

Shazeer and Jumper's 2026 departures from Google DeepMind (to OpenAI/Anthropic) are cited as contributing factors in Gemini 3.5 Pro's delay — a concrete, dated example of the talent war having a direct product consequence, not just a headline.
