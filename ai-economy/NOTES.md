## Course shape — restructured into 3 units, 16 lessons (2026-07-25)
- After L0001–L0004 (the original flat, organically-grown sequence), the user pushed back explicitly: "I don't think the course right now is very systematic," and asked what a from-scratch course overview would look like with "a more natural scaffolding and grouping of ideas."
- Response: redesigned around three units, each a genuinely different axis rather than a flat numbered list — **Unit A: The Economic Structure** (who's in the industry, how money/labor move — A1–A7), **Unit B: The Technology Stack** (what's actually being built, ordered bottom-up: models → data → retrieval/tools → agents → governance → dev process → distribution — B1–B7), **Unit C: Reading the Discourse** (meta-skills for after the course ends — C1–C2). Old L0001–L0004 content was mapped/split into new slots rather than discarded (e.g. old L0003 ≈ new B4 nearly unchanged; old L0004 split cleanly into B5 + B6).
- The user then asked to fold in more depth on **tooling & service providers as a "fourth player"** (their words) alongside labs/infra/capital — specifically named examples: harnesses ("pi"), workflow platforms (n8n), agents ("OpenClaw") — plus a new **labor-market chapter** (FDEs and how hiring has changed). This grew Unit A from 4 to 7 lessons and is the origin of A2/A3/A5.
- **Key correction from the user, worth generalizing**: after proposing lessons titled around single vendors (AI-DLC specifically, Hugging Face specifically), the user flagged that this implicitly frames one company's product as the industry default. Rule going forward for this workspace: **when a lesson covers a category that has real competitors, survey the category and place the "obvious" name within it — never title or frame a lesson around a single vendor's product as if it were the unchallenged standard.** This produced B6 (AI-DLC placed alongside GitHub Spec Kit, Microsoft's Agentic DevOps, Thoughtworks' Agent/works, plus honest adoption-survey data showing most teams use none of them formally) and B7 (Hugging Face's real, bifurcated position — dominant at the model-hub layer, a minor player at the inference-serving layer next to Fireworks/Together/Groq/OpenRouter). This fairness check is worth applying proactively to any future lesson before writing it, not just when corrected.
- Quiz design: mix free-recall and multiple-choice from the start (no evidence yet this user finds 3-option recognition quizzes too easy, unlike the mythology-course user, but default to the harder mix as a safe starting point until we see how it lands).
- Each new research pass in this course has surfaced at least one "don't trust stale knowledge" showcase fact worth keeping as a teaching device (AGI's contractual stakes; the Fivetran/dbt/GX consolidation; OPA's Apple/Styra shakeup) — deliberately highlight one of these per lesson where the research turns one up, since they make the course's own sourcing discipline concrete rather than abstract.
